    Users · account management
    
    
    
      Accounts that sign in to the studio. Elder — a senior babaláwo who
      authors and verifies others' work. Babaláwo — a contributor: authors
      verses, interpretations and intonations, which land as drafts for an elder to verify.
      A contributor never verifies their own work. Passwords are set here but never shown —
      use Reset password to issue a new one.
